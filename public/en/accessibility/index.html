Accessibility statement

Updated: 5 November 2026

Our approach

The site is built to make its information and contact paths clear and accessible. The development target is WCAG 2.1 AA, with reference to Israeli Standard 5568; this is not a certification.

Site features

Semantic headings, keyboard navigation, a skip link, visible focus, field labels and form feedback. Layouts adapt to screen widths and respect reduced motion. Contact details and capability content are available without running the interactions. Search, topic selection and the interactive form require JavaScript; you can also contact us by phone.

Testing and enquiries

The site has been checked at desktop and phone sizes, including the enquiry flow. A professional audit across every device and screen-reader combination has not been completed. For help, contact accessibility coordinator Shahar at shahar@keystone.org.il or 050-564-0429. Include the page, action and difficulty; medical information is not needed. You may also use the contact form.
