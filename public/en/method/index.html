KEYSTONE / OUR METHOD

A capability has to work.
Inside your organization.

Start with a organization goal. Build a capability you can operate, assess and improve.

01

Discovery

Define what the organization needs to make possible. Review existing tools, data access and capacity.

02

Proof

Choose a workflow and acceptance criteria. Test with organization-approved data, including exceptions and failures.

03

Implementation

Connect the tools, assign responsibility and approvals, and prepare the team for everyday work and disruptions.

04

Growth

Measure outcomes, improve the system and expand capabilities based on what the organization learns.
