Book an intro call How we work

In a stone arch, the keystone is the last stone set in place. It is the one that holds all the others.

That is the role we take in your business. Not one more piece of software to maintain, but the link between the people, tools and processes you already have, so everything holds together and runs without wasted effort.

We start with the work itself: where the time goes, what gets typed twice, what depends on one person remembering. Then we build exactly what is needed.

What we do

Three areas, one question in each: what will save you the most time and the most mistakes.

  • Workflow and automation

    We sit with you, map how work flows today and find where it gets stuck: double typing, copying between systems, tracking in spreadsheets, manual reminders. What can be automated, we automate.

    • Documents, quotes and invoices
    • Data synced between systems
    • Customer reminders and follow-ups
    • Reports that update themselves

  • Business systems

    A customer management system, an internal tool for your team, connections between the software you already use, or an AI assistant that answers recurring questions. We build what the business needs, not what is easiest to sell.

    • Customer and inquiry management
    • Internal tools for your team
    • Connections between existing software
    • AI assistants

  • Websites

    Fast, secure and accessible websites, built around what your customers need to find and do. Every site meets the Israeli accessibility standard and includes a privacy policy.

    • Company and service websites
    • Forms that feed straight into your systems
    • Accessibility to IS 5568
    • Security and privacy by design

How we work

Four stages. At the end of each one you know exactly where things stand.

  1. Learn

    We meet, understand the business and what is weighing on it, map the processes and work out what they cost in time and money today.

  2. Plan

    You get a clear document: what we will build, why, what it costs and when it will be ready. No surprises later.

  3. Build

    We build in short stages and show progress along the way, so the direction can be adjusted in time.

  4. Support

    We train your team, document everything and stay available for support and improvements.
