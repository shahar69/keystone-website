Privacy policy

Updated: 5 November 2026

Who collects information, and why?

Keystone collects information submitted through this site to respond to enquiries, arrange a conversation and manage the relationship with the enquirer. Providing details is voluntary; without contact details we cannot respond. For privacy enquiries: shahar@keystone.org.il or 050-564-0429.

Information submitted in a form

The form includes your name, company or organization, contact details and enquiry. Topics selected in the capability library may appear in the enquiry text; you can edit or remove them before submitting. The form information is sent when you submit it and is stored with its language and source. An enquiry does not subscribe you to marketing. Do not submit passwords, client information or sensitive medical or financial information through the public form.

Local storage and enquiry continuity

A random identifier and selected topics are stored in browser sessionStorage to connect actions in the same tab and preserve context when moving to the enquiry form. They are not advertising identifiers. This local storage ends when the tab closes. No account is required to contact Keystone.

Tools and public website checks

If you run a website check, its address is sent to Keystone’s server to read the public page and a limited link sample. The check does not sign in or submit forms on the checked site. Findings may be retained in the enquiry record; the private report link expires after seven days. Anyone holding the link can access the report, so share it only with intended readers. Do not enter private links or addresses containing credentials. External tools, if offered, will include the relevant information and consent before use.

Access and service providers

Access to enquiries is limited to personnel authorized to handle them. Hosting, email and communication providers may process information to operate the site and handle an enquiry, depending on the services enabled. Some providers may process information outside Israel. A WhatsApp or other external-service link takes you to a service governed by its own policy. Keystone does not sell the information submitted through this site.

Measurement, protection and retention

The site stores aggregate usage by page and day, without a personal profile or advertising cookie. An IP address is used in memory for abuse prevention and is not attached to the enquiry. Infrastructure logs may include technical information. Access controls and input validation are used. Enquiries are generally retained for up to 730 days for follow-up and relationship management, unless law or an ongoing matter requires otherwise. Email copies, exports and backups may have separate retention. A necessary sign-in cookie is used only in the administration area.

Access, correction and enquiries

You may contact us to request access to your information or correction, and request deletion under applicable law. Requests are reviewed after appropriate identity verification. This policy may be updated as the services change; the update date appears on this page.
